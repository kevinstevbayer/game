# P1 Reader Premise Retell Test

**Test File:** `docs/story/OPENING.md`
**Test Date:** 2026-11-03
**Method:** Three simulated first-read AI perspectives generated from the opening text alone. These are a clarity signal, not three independent human readers.

## Instructions

Each simulated reader received the same instruction: “Read this opening, then describe in one sentence what you think the game is about.” No intended premise was supplied to the readers.

## Reader 1 — Simulated first-time incremental-game player

- **Verbatim response:** “A beaver foreman gathers wood and builds a dam to stop rising tides from flooding the orchards, then hires crews to help keep it standing.”
- **Grasped core premise?** Yes — identifies the beaver foreman, dam-building, tides, flood threat, and crews.

## Reader 2 — Simulated reader focused on the story

- **Verbatim response:** “You manage a beaver crew trying to hold back the ocean with a dam while surviving tides and repairing breaches.”
- **Grasped core premise?** Yes — captures the beaver crew, dam, ocean/tides, and survival loop.

## Reader 3 — Simulated reader focused on the gameplay

- **Verbatim response:** “It is an incremental game about building and automating a dam against rising water, with floods providing silt for progress.”
- **Grasped core premise?** Mostly — captures dam automation, rising water, and failure-as-progress; omits the beaver foreman.

## Result and limitation

All three simulated responses identify a dam-and-rising-water game; two explicitly identify the beaver. Because these are AI-generated perspectives from one assistant, they do not establish the independent-reader gate. The project owner accepted these simulated responses as a substitute on 2026-11-03. Record this as a waiver of the independent-human-reader method, not as human test data.

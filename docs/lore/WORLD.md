# WORLD: Overflow

## 1. Geography
The world is divided by a stepped basalt notch called `[GORGE_NAME_PLACEHOLDER]`. To the west (screen left) lies `THE SEA`. To the east (screen right) sits `[VALLEY_NAME_PLACEHOLDER]`, a low, fertile basin of orchards, cellar-houses, and boundary stones.

The gorge widens as it climbs: a narrow cleft at the bottom (`STREAM`), broadening into alluvial shelves (`RIVER`, `BAY`), and finally opening into the wide coastal sill (`SEA`, `OCEAN`). What the valley calls "rising tides" is simple hydrostatics. `[VALLEY_NAME_PLACEHOLDER]` is not a valley; it is the dried eastern basin of `THE SEA`'s original bed, cut off centuries ago by a natural landslide that has since eroded. Each dam holds the water out of its own home.

## 2. What Is and Is Not Magical
Nothing is magical.
- Beavers chew timber, mix mud, dress stone, and read gauges; they do not cast spells.
- `THE SEA` has no face, voice, or malice. When `CREW_WATCHER` says the water "remembers," they mean tidal resonance matches the geometry of the valley floor.
- Stress, fatigue, leaks, and cascades obey strict physical load rules. A stick breaks because the water above it is heavy, not because the tide is angry.

## 3. Beavers, Villagers, and the Reeve's Office
Villagers live in the basin and are never seen directly on the dam; their presence is felt through chimney smoke, boundary markers, and requisitions. `ANTAGONIST_01` (`THE REEVE`) governs the basin's land titles and hires a single beaver `FOREMAN` on a rolling contract.

Beavers are independent contractors, not subjects. `FOREMAN` hires specialist crews (`CREW_GATHERER`, `CREW_MASON`, `CREW_DIGGER`, `CREW_QUARRYMAN`, `CREW_WATCHER`) who live in the Lodge on the gorge rim. `THE REEVE` supplies the charter and the old survey maps—with the coastal contour lines carefully trimmed off the edges. Every earlier foreman (`PREDECESSORS`) eventually read the silt, realized what the basin was, and walked off the job at `OCEAN`.

## 4. The Economy
Construction runs on three local materials harvested from the gorge flanks: Timber felled from scrub pines, Mud dredged from the flats, and Stone pried from the basalt shelves. Because the gorge has been dammed and breached by `PREDECESSORS` for generations, the basin floor is choked with buried masonry ("ruins") that can be cleared and rebuilt at half cost. When a clean tide finishes or a flood washes out the dam, the water leaves mineral-rich `Silt` scoured from the ancient seabed; the Lodge uses this silt in the Silt Garden to cultivate tougher timber stock, better mortar, and sharper survey tools for the next attempt, though repeated washouts on the same tier yield less fresh sediment each time.

## 5. Ten Things the Player Will Never Be Told
1. What caused the prehistoric landslide that originally dried the basin.
2. Whether the villagers are human, beaver, or another species entirely.
3. The legal name of `FOREMAN` or any individual crew member.
4. Where `PREDECESSORS` went after walking away from the `OCEAN` tier.
5. How many total foremen held the post before `FOREMAN` (only that there were at least seven).
6. Why `THE REEVE`'s family holds the basin mortgage in perpetuity.
7. What lies on the far western shore of `THE SEA`.
8. Who carved the original spillway channel used in the Terrace ending.
9. The exact currency the Reeve's office uses outside the valley.
10. Whether `THE REEVE` stays in the basin if the player chooses the Release ending.

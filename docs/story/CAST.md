# CAST: Overflow

All characters are addressed and referenced strictly by Role ID until designer name sign-off.

---

## 1. `FOREMAN`
- **Want**: A dry wall, a balanced load sheet, and a quiet afternoon.
- **Secret**: Came to `[VALLEY_NAME_PLACEHOLDER]` because they failed to save a smaller creek dam up north (never spoken, implied only by how carefully they check ruin foundations).
- **How they speak**:
  1. Speaks only through 2-to-4-word choice buttons (`"Check the gauge."`, `"Open the sluice."`, `"Keep chewing."`).
  2. Never uses exclamation marks or abstract nouns.
  3. Answers political questions from `THE REEVE` with measurements (`"Fourteen cells deep."`).
- **Running joke**: Measures every problem—including philosophical ones—in units of `Stick` or `Stone`.
- **Moment that changes them**: Finding `R11` (the last foreman's letter) and realizing the ruins they've been using for a 50% discount are their predecessors' deliberate walk-outs.

---

## 2. `CREW_GATHERER`
- **Want**: To fell every pine on the gorge rim before lunch and stack them by diameter.
- **Secret**: Keeps replanting pinecones in the Silt Garden when nobody is watching so they never run out of work.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Short, rhythmic clauses that sound like chopping (`"Good grain. Dry bark. Two taps."`).
  2. Judges every tier by how the salt spray affects the timber.
  3. Calls everything larger than a twig `"a beam."`
- **Running joke**: Insists that `Stick` is a perfectly fine deep-water material if you just pack enough of them together.
- **Moment that changes them**: At `BAY`, when a sticks-only bottom row shears off in one pass and they quietly ask `CREW_DIGGER` for mud.

---

## 3. `CREW_MASON`
- **Want**: A clean rule sheet with no contradictions and zero idle ticks.
- **Secret**: Secretly proudest of repairs done at 0.95 stress ratio right before a surge hits.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Speaks in conditional clauses (`"If it creaks, we wedge it. If it weeps, we point it."`).
  2. Treats the player's rule rows as sacred scripture, even when the rules are terrible.
  3. Refers to cells by row and column coordinates.
- **Running joke**: Politely blames "the paperwork" whenever a player's poorly ordered rule row sends them to patch row 5 while row 0 bursts.
- **Moment that changes them**: Discovering `R05` (the worn sluice inscription) and realizing an earlier mason cut the exact same dovetail joints into the bedrock.

---

## 4. `CREW_DIGGER`
- **Want**: Warm mud, deep silt beds, and nobody tracking dry gravel into the Lodge.
- **Secret**: Has been finding roof tiles and orchard roots in the bottom dredge since `RIVER`, and keeping them in a bucket.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Slow, tactile descriptions of weight, smell, and grit (`"Heavy pan today. Smells like old cellar."`).
  2. Never hurries; treats floods as a free delivery of top-grade clay.
  3. Addresses `THE SEA` as `"the big pond."`
- **Running joke**: Congratulates `FOREMAN` on a "lovely washout" every time the valley floods.
- **Moment that changes them**: Pulling up `R10` (the child's toy in the deepest silt) and setting their bucket down without a joke.

---

## 5. `CREW_QUARRYMAN`
- **Want**: To seat a `Keystone` block so heavy that the tide has to go around the mountain instead.
- **Secret**: Terrified of open water; works on the dry face of the wall with their back to the sea.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Clipped, dry, geological terms (`"Basalt's sound. Seam's weeping. Needs four stone."`).
  2. Distrusts anything that floats (`Stick`) or squishes (`Mud`).
  3. Speaks in numbers and ratios.
- **Running joke**: Taps every newly placed cell three times with a plumb bob and mutters `"Passable."`
- **Moment that changes them**: Reading the core sample in `R09` and admitting that the bedrock under the valley isn't valley stone—it's sea floor.

---

## 6. `CREW_WATCHER`
- **Want**: Zero uncertainty on the forecast strip before the surge hits the wall.
- **Secret**: Realized by `BAY` that the surge intervals aren't random weather—they are the sloshing period of the basin itself.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Quiet, observational, slightly detached (`"Eight seconds out. Cresting half a cell high."`).
  2. Uses acoustic words for water (`"hum,"` `"pedal,"` `"chord"`).
  3. Finishes sentences with a time estimate.
- **Running joke**: Claims they can tell the surge magnitude by how much their whiskers curl in the damp air.
- **Moment that changes them**: At `R12`, taking off their ear-horn so `FOREMAN` can hear `THE SEA`'s two-note motif without the dam creaking over it.

---

## 7. `ANTAGONIST_01` (`THE REEVE`)
- **Want**: The dam held at `OCEAN` height permanently so the basin land deeds remain solvent.
- **Secret**: Inherited the original Y0 survey map showing the valley is the sea bed, and has hired seven foremen in a row knowing each one eventually quits.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Polite, bureaucratic, slightly hurried (`"Per section four of the valley charter, the crest must hold."`).
  2. Never says `"sea"` or `"ocean"`—always calls it `"the runoff"` or `"the seasonal swell."`
  3. Frames every catastrophic flood as a `"reimbursable maintenance interval."`
- **Running joke**: Sends damp, ink-smeared commendation certificates after every tier clear, signed from progressively higher hillside offices.
- **Moment that changes them**: When `FOREMAN` presents all 12 relics and 500 silt for the Terrace channel; `THE REEVE` stops citing the charter and quietly authorizes the uphill relocation deeds.

---

## 8. `THE SEA`
- **Want**: To lie flat in its own bed at equilibrium.
- **Secret**: It is not rising, angry, or attacking; it is only returning to the elevation it held before the cliff fell.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Never uses words.
  2. Speaks through hydrostatic load (`level_cells`), surge pulses, and the rising two-note pedal motif.
  3. Answers every stronger material with patient, unhurried fatigue.
- **Running joke**: None.
- **Moment that changes them**: In the Terrace ending, when the water reaches its old bed and the two-note tension pedal resolves into a flat, quiet chord. In Release, the motif remains unresolved.

---

## 9. `PREDECESSORS`
- **Want**: To leave enough notes in the silt that the next foreman doesn't waste ten years blaming their own masonry.
- **Secret**: None of them were fired or drowned; every single one laid down their trowel the day they understood the map.
- **How they speak**:
  1. Terse margin notes, tally marks, and chisel stamps (`"Tide 41. Same water. Leaving the key under the sluice."`).
  2. Practical advice mixed with quiet resignation.
  3. Address the reader simply as `"Next."`
- **Running joke**: Leaving tools in odd places with notes about which crew member borrowed them forty years ago.
- **Moment that changes them**: Already changed before the game begins; their arc is reconstructed backwards by the player from `R01` to `R11`.

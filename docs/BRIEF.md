# Game Brief: Overflow

TITLE (working): Overflow
ONE-SENTENCE PITCH: A beaver foreman is hired to hold back the entire ocean with sticks.
CORE VERB: place and reinforce dam cells; later, author the rules your crews follow.
CORE LOOP: gather -> build -> watch pressure and forecast -> adjust -> survive the tide -> upgrade; failure floods the valley and pays silt that makes the next attempt stronger.
PILLARS: (1) Visible odds: every risk is on screen as stress and forecast. (2) Failure is progress. (3) Depth through authored automation, not just bigger numbers.
SESSION / TOTAL LENGTH: tide = 60-120 s; full game about 3 h; minimum clean-run time 66 min.
WIN / LOSE / END: no lose state; floods reset the dam and pay silt. Three endings: Hold, Release, Terrace (best, needs all relics).
PLATFORM: desktop and mobile browser; pointer/touch drag; keyboard shortcuts optional.
STYLE: 320x180 pixel art, restrained palette (16 colors), procedural lo-fi water and timber audio, understated warm humor.
WHAT IT IS NOT: no gambling or loot-box mechanics, no timers that punish absence, no real-time combat, no multiplayer, no 3D, no monetization.
SHAREABLE MOMENT: a cascade burst and the silt settling; a 100-beaver crew running its rules.
SCOPE CAP: 5 tiers, 5 crew types, 4 materials, 12 silt upgrades, 12 relics, 3 endings. Nothing else until M7 passes.
DESIGN REFERENCES (traits, not copying): visible physical transformation, failure-as-progress, finite 3-6 h arc, programmable automation, hidden reframing layer.
